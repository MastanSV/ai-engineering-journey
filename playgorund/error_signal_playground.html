The Error Signal

Don't calculate a whole backpropagation pass yet. Just watch one error signal travel backward.

Play with the signal

Change a weight or turn the ReLU gate on/off.

How to read it

Start at the right. The red signal comes from the loss. Follow it toward the left.

At each weight, the signal gets scaled. At ReLU, the gate either lets it through or stops it.
